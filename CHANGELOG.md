# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 25 November 2026

First public research release of the Visor AI Chat Monetization Dataset v1 by Visor Research / the Visor team.

- 20,080 synthetic, deduplicated US-English chat messages
- Official IAB Content Taxonomy 3.0 tier-1 and tier-2 labels (70 tier-2 classes)
- commercial_intent, funnel_stage, brand_safe, ad_eligible
- Stratified train / validation split (18,072 / 2,008)
- Documentation: dataset card, labels, stats, citations, schema.org JSON-LD
- License: MIT (dataset messages, labels, and Visor documentation)
